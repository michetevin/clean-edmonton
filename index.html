Service disclaimers

  • Stains: Results vary by stain type and age. Ink, dye, bleach, burn marks, rust and long-set stains may not be fully removable. We'll advise you before starting.
  • Odours: Pet odour treatment is highly effective but severe or long-standing contamination that has penetrated foam, padding or subfloor may require repeat treatments.
  • Fabric: Some fabrics are delicate, prone to shrinkage, colour bleed, pilling or browning. We follow manufacturer codes where available and always test first — but pre-existing wear, fading or damage may become more visible after cleaning.
  • Drying: Typical dry time is 4–6 hours but depends on fabric, humidity, airflow and temperature. Please allow adequate drying time before use.
  • Warranty & wear: Cleaning does not repair structural damage, broken frames, worn seams, sagging cushions or failed foam.
  • Pricing: All prices shown are starting rates in CAD and may be adjusted after on-site assessment of size, fabric, soiling and access.
